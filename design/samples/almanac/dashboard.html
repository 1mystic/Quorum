The hazard ratios are computed but not shown.

Blocking check failed — “hazards stay proportional over time”. Schoenfeld residual test, global p = 0.003, and wing D alone p < 0.001. Wing D was slow for the first six weeks after its pump replacement and normal afterwards, so a single ratio would average two different worlds into one misleading number.

A ratio of 1.9× would look decisive on a slide and would be wrong. The value is suppressed rather than footnoted, because a footnote is not read aloud in a committee meeting.

What would fix it: a stratified model per wing, or a time-varying coefficient. Both are on this method's card.
