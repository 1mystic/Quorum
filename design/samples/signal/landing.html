02 — THE SHADOW

A number should cast the width of its own doubt.

Most of this product is uncertainty, not values. So in this direction an interval is a solid block sitting behind its estimate — you can see how wide the doubt is from across the room, which is where most of these screens are actually read from.

READ IT FROM THE BACK ROW

Wide doubt looks heavy. Narrow doubt looks light.

Confidence bands are hard-edged offset blocks, not soft washes. A wide interval is physically a bigger shape than a narrow one, so the eye ranks certainty before it reads a digit.

Control limits get their own language — a hard rule with a cross-hatched exceedance zone — because a limit is a decision boundary, not an estimate, and a committee that confuses the two makes a bad decision confidently.

05 — THE METHOD

From your record to a number you can defend.

01

Map, don't rebuild

A vertical manifest maps your entities onto the six streams. Complaints and club tasks both become request_flow. Nothing is written twice.

02

Every method declares its floor

30 events for a survival curve. 20 periods for control limits. 5 members for any published per-stratum cell — a hard k-anonymity floor that overrides even a tenant admin asking to see it.

03

Nothing crosses as a bare float

Every figure travels in an evidence envelope: value, n, interval, interval kind, method id, and the assumption checks the service ran on its own output. A blocking failure suppresses the value and shows the reason in its place.

04

The model narrates. That's all.

The assistant's tools return envelopes. It may quote a value, its interval and its n, and explain a failing check. It may not do arithmetic on two envelopes or state a figure that is not in one — enforced by a grounding test, not by a polite prompt.
