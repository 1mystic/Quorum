§ 02 · Thesis

Every other tool shows you a number. We show you how much of one it is.

“Average resolution: 3.2 days” is not a fact, it is a summary of a choice. Which tickets were counted, which were quietly dropped, and how wide the honest answer really is.

The bug is in every dashboard you have used.

Average resolution time is usually computed over closed tickets only. But a ticket that is still open has an unknown resolution time — and the ones still open are exactly the slow ones. Dropping them does not make the number neutral. It biases it downward, every single time.

Quorum censors instead of dropping. The 44 open requests above contribute what is known about them — that they have already exceeded their current age — and the curve is honest as a result.

Same discipline everywhere else. A vendor at 3-for-3 does not outrank one at 47-for-52. A forecast that cannot beat a seasonal-naive baseline does not ship. A “30% risk” bucket is checked against whether 30% of it actually defaulted.

§ 04 · How

Three steps, and the third one is the product.

01

Onboard by slug

Pick a vertical. t/vaikunth-heights gets a housing society's labels, categories and roles; t/aavartan-robotics gets a club's. Same schema, different manifest.

02

Run the community

Requests, dues, events, announcements, polls. Ordinary operations software that people will actually open — because the boring part has to work before the clever part matters.

03

Read what it means

Every figure arrives with its sample size, its interval and its assumption checks. When a method cannot yet speak, it says what it is waiting for instead of guessing.
