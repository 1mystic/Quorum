What failed

Schoenfeld residual test: global p = 0.003, wing D alone p < 0.001.

Wing D was slow for six weeks after its pump replacement and ordinary afterwards. A single hazard ratio would average two different regimes into one number that reads as decisive and is not.

Remedies on the method card: stratify by wing, or fit a time-varying coefficient. Neither is chosen automatically, because that choice changes what the number means.
