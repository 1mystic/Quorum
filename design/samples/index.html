The house direction

Synthesis · recommended

Quorum

Graticule's instrument geometry and section system, rebuilt on Almanac's comfortable spacing — then given a palette and type that do not read as 2019. Warm limestone canvas, spruce green, apricot accent. Bricolage Grotesque for display, Inter Tight for text, JetBrains Mono carrying every number.

The dashboard is the real change: bigger numbers, far fewer words on screen, and every long explanation moved behind a Why disclosure instead of sitting in a paragraph. Buttons fill on hover rather than just changing colour.

Landing →Dashboard →

What changed from the feedback: Graticule's alignment inconsistencies fixed onto a strict 8pt grid; section headers restacked; the dashboard decluttered with progressive disclosure and a larger type baseline; palette and type refreshed away from Almanac's expected cream-and-terracotta.
