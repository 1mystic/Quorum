Writing good announcements

1 Keep the title under 10 words. Members scan titles first before reading the body.
2 State the who, what, when, and where in the first two sentences.
3 Use the correct category so students can filter by topic in their feed.
4 Pin only when the information is time-sensitive. Over-pinning reduces impact.
5 All 84 members of your club will see this in their Announcements feed immediately.
! Announcements cannot be edited after posting. Delete and re-post if corrections are needed.
